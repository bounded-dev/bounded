We run a little tool library on our street — shared things people can borrow
instead of everyone buying their own: a couple of drills, a ladder, a
pressure washer, a wallpaper steamer, that kind of thing. Right now it's run
off a scrappy notebook and it's chaos. We lose track of who has what, and
last week two people turned up for the same ladder on the same afternoon.

I'd like something the whole street can use to sort this out.

People should be able to look at everything in the library and tell at a
glance what's there and whether each thing is free or already out with
someone. When they take something, they put it out under their name; when
they bring it back, they mark it returned and it's available for the next
person. And it has to remember all of this for everyone between visits — if I
take the ladder out this morning, my neighbour looking this evening should
see it's already gone and who has it.

The obvious mix-ups should just be impossible: you can't take something
that's already out with someone else, you can't return something that was
never taken or that isn't ours, and the record of who has borrowed what
should always add up — nothing lost, nothing counted twice. It would help to
see a bit of history too, so we can gently chase whoever's had the pressure
washer for three weeks.
